The 5-Step GEO Framework.Become the agent AI recommends.

When a buyer asks ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity "who's the best agent near me?", the answer is three to six names. Everyone else isn't there. Across AI tools, the source those names are pulled from most is the agent's Google Business Profile. This is the exact system to become one of the names.

01

Claim your entity

Lock one consistent name, role, market, and specialty everywhere AI looks: Google Business Profile, your website, your socials, your brokerage page, Zillow, Realtor.com, Yelp, Bing.

Why: AI builds your profile from these. Conflicting or thin info makes you invisible.

02

Feed the 5 signals

Complete your Google Business Profile (description, Services, photos), stack Google and Zillow reviews, and keep publishing GEO content.

Why: Google retired profile Q&A. Its AI now describes you to buyers from what's on your profile, your reviews and your site. Your Google profile is the most-cited source across AI tools, and Zillow's agent pages are what ChatGPT links to most.

03

Answer the questions

Write content that matches how buyers ask AI in full sentences, like "best first-time buyer agent in [your city]," with a real fact and where it came from.

Why: answer the exact question and AI starts quoting you by name. Pages that only say "I'm the best" don't get cited.

04

Build authority

Earn mentions on the local pages, directories, and publications AI already trusts.

Why: a few of the right citations outrank a hundred of the wrong ones.

05

Compound daily

Run the 30-second daily move that stacks small signals into authority over a season.

Why: consistent beats big. The agents who show up daily win the recommendation.

The Always-On Agent System
Step 01 tool · Your entity statement

The exact words AI needs.

The template

[Full Name], REALTOR®, is a licensed real estate agent in [State] serving [City / Area]. [He / She / They] work with [buyer or seller type] looking for [need or price range].

Real example

Sarah Chen, REALTOR®, is a licensed real estate agent in Minnesota serving Plymouth and the northwest suburbs. She works with first-time buyers searching for homes under $450K.

Paste it word for word into your Google Business Profile description, your website bio, Zillow, Realtor.com, and every social profile. Same words, everywhere.

Step 02 tool · The signals

The 5 signals to feed.

Google reviews — grow the count, keep them recent. Ask every closed client to name the city and what they bought. Reply to every one.
Zillow reviews — grow the count, keep them recent. Zillow's agent pages are the pages ChatGPT links to most.
A complete Google profile — a plain-sentence description, 5 to 8 specific Services, 10+ real photos. Google's AI answers buyers' questions from this now that Q&A is gone.
GEO content — publish answers to real buyer questions, with a sourced fact, consistently.
A consistent entity — same name, role, and market across the web: your site, Google, Zillow, Realtor.com, Yelp, Bing Places.
Five steps. One outcome.

Become one of the names AI gives when a buyer asks, "who is the best agent near me?"
